ABSTRACT 
The recruitment process has become increasingly competitive, with organizations receiving a 
large number of resumes for a single job position. At the same time, students and job seekers 
often struggle to determine whether their resumes adequately represent their skills and match 
the requirements of a particular job. Traditional resume screening and basic keyword-based 
Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) may fail to capture the semantic relationship between a 
candidate's skills, experience, projects, and the requirements specified in a job description. This 
creates a need for an intelligent system that can analyze resumes more comprehensively and 
provide meaningful, personalized feedback. 
This project proposes an AI-Powered Resume Analysis and Job Matching System designed 
to assist candidates in evaluating and improving their resumes. The system accepts a resume in 
formats such as PDF or DOCX and extracts relevant information including educational 
qualifications, technical skills, work experience, projects, certifications, and other professional 
details. Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques are then used to process and structure 
the extracted information. 
A key component of the proposed system is job-resume matching. Users can provide a target 
job description, after which the system identifies important skills, qualifications, technologies, 
and experience expected for the role. Semantic similarity techniques and text embeddings are 
used to compare the requirements of the job description with the information present in the 
candidate's resume. Based on this comparison, the system generates an overall compatibility 
score and identifies matching as well as missing skills. This provides a more meaningful 
assessment than simple keyword matching by considering the contextual similarity between the 
resume and job requirements. 
The proposed system therefore combines Natural Language Processing, text embeddings, 
semantic similarity, information extraction, and Generative AI into a single application. The 
objective is not to replace professional recruiters, but to provide students and job seekers with 
an accessible tool for understanding how effectively their resumes align with specific career 
opportunities. The resulting system aims to make resume evaluation more informative, 
personalized, and actionable while demonstrating the practical application of modern Artificial 
Intelligence techniques to a real-world recruitment problem.
